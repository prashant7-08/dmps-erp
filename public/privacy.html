1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications, web portals, School ERP systems, biometric synchronization utilities, and cloud services operated and published by PKR ENTERPRISES (including "Dadheech Memorial Public School Portal", "Dadheech ERP", "PKR EduTech", and "PKR Smart Campus OS"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of students, parents, teachers, and school administrative staff.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to provide academic, administrative, and fee management services:

  • Student & Guardian Information: Name, class, section, admission number, roll number, date of birth, guardian phone number, email, and residential address.
  • Academic & Attendance Records: Biometric attendance logs, daily attendance status, examination marks, grading reports, and homework submissions.
  • Financial & Fee Records: Tuition fee structures, bus transport fee dues, payment transaction IDs, and digital fee receipts. (We do not store credit card/debit card numbers or bank passwords; all transactions are processed securely via RBI-licensed payment gateways).
  • Device & Usage Data: Device model, OS version, push notification tokens, and crash diagnostics to ensure app stability and delivery of real-time attendance alerts.

3. Device Permissions Requested

Our mobile applications may request the following device permissions solely for functional purposes:

  • Camera / Photos: To allow parents and teachers to upload student profile pictures, homework attachments, and scan fee receipt QR codes.
  • Notifications: To deliver real-time WhatsApp and push alerts for student arrival, departure, fee due reminders, and school circulars.
  • Storage: To download fee receipts, examination report cards, syllabus PDFs, and timetable documents.

4. How We Use & Protect Your Information

  • Zero Data Selling: We NEVER sell, rent, or trade any personal or academic data to third-party marketing or advertising networks.
  • End-to-End Encryption: All data transmitted between the client application and our cloud databases is encrypted using 256-bit SSL/TLS protocol.
  • Role-Based Access: Access to student records is strictly restricted to authenticated school administrators, authorized class teachers, and verified parents for their respective wards only.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Academic records are retained as mandated by the affiliated education board and respective school management guidelines. Parents or guardians wishing to request correction or deletion of their contact details can contact their school administrative office or email our Data Protection Officer at prashant732009@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & IT Act Compliance)

Our applications are designed for educational institutions. Student profiles are created and managed directly by the authorized school administrative staff with parental consent. We adhere to all provisions of the Indian Information Technology Act and global best practices for minors' data safety.
